One Power Pilot

Wind, solar, and wave energy inputs connected to hydrogen storage and verified reporting.

In partnership with One Power, ResonanceDAO is developing a pilot architecture that links electrolyzer-based productive energy hardware with a Green Energy Verification layer for renewable input, hydrogen output, uptime, avoided-emissions assumptions, savings, carbon-reduction support, and revenue flows.

The goal is not to financialize climate claims prematurely. The goal is to make infrastructure performance legible enough for grants, financiers, communities, and operators to trust the same operating record.

Timelock for Wallets

Governance-approved wallet actions are queued, delayed, checked against bounded dependencies, and released only after approval, dispute, and cancel paths are satisfied.

✓Governance-approved transaction bundles are queued before release.
