One Power Pilot

A pilot for hydrogen-powered renewable infrastructure, carbon reduction, and electricity-transition savings.

In partnership with One Power, ResonanceDAO is developing a pilot architecture that links electrolyzer-based productive energy hardware with a proof layer for renewable input, hydrogen output, uptime, avoided-emissions assumptions, savings, carbon-reduction support, and revenue flows.

The goal is not to financialize climate claims prematurely. The goal is to make infrastructure performance legible enough for grants, financiers, communities, and operators to trust the same operating record.
