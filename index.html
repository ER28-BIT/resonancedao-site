One Power Pilot

Wind, solar, and wave energy inputs connected to hydrogen storage and verified reporting.

In partnership with One Power, ResonanceDAO is developing a pilot architecture that links productive energy hardware with a Green Energy Verification layer for renewable input, hydrogen output, uptime, avoided-emissions assumptions, savings, and infrastructure reporting.

The goal is not to financialize climate claims prematurely. The goal is to make infrastructure performance legible enough for grants, financiers, public-sector partners, communities, and operators to trust the same operating record.
